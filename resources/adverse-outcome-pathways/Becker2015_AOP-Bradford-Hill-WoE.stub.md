## Abstract (paraphrased from verified search-result record)
Proposes a systematic, transparent method for assessing scientific confidence in AOPs using **tailored Bradford-Hill considerations**: biological plausibility, essentiality of key events, and empirical support (dose-response concordance, temporal concordance, incidence concordance). Biological plausibility and empirical support are evaluated for each **Key Event Relationship (KER)** individually; **essentiality** is evaluated for each KE in the context of the whole AOP. Each consideration receives a high/strong, moderate, or low/weak weight-of-evidence rating. The paper also describes a prototype quantitative multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) model for AOP confidence and illustrates application with six human-health and ecological AOP examples.
