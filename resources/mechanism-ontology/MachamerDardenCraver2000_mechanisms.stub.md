## Core definition (verbatim, MDC 2000: 3)
> "Mechanisms are entities and activities organized such that they are productive of
> regular changes from start or set-up to finish or termination conditions."

## Key framework (the "new mechanist" dualist ontology)
- A mechanism has two irreducible kinds of components:
  - **Entities** — the things (parts) that engage in activities; they have
    properties that enable the activities. (Maps to BFO material entities / their
    qualities.)
  - **Activities** — the producers of change; the "doings" / causings. Activities
    are not reducible to mere properties; causation is *productive*, not just
    constant conjunction. (Maps to BFO processes / occurrents.)
- **Organization**: entities and activities are spatially, temporally, and causally
  organized so the mechanism runs from **set-up (start) conditions** through stages
  to **termination (finish) conditions** — productive continuity, no gaps.
- **Regularity**: mechanisms work the same way under the same conditions (this
  grounds the *abbreviation* of a mechanism by a single causal edge).
- Mechanistic explanation = describing the entities + activities + organization that
  produce a phenomenon; it replaces law-based explanation in molecular biology and
  neuroscience.

## RELEVANCE to PhysioMap
- Philosophical warrant for the central PhysioMap move: a **within-scale causal edge
  ABBREVIATES a mechanism**. Because mechanisms are *regular* (MDC), a recurring
  entities+activities organization can be summarized as one A->B edge with a
  mechanism reference, without re-listing every entity and activity each time.
- The MDC entities/activities duality maps cleanly onto the BFO continuant/occurrent
  duality and onto GO-CAM's (gene-product-as-entity, molecular-activity) graphs:
  PhysioMap's mechanism ref is a pointer to an entities+activities organization.
- "Set-up -> termination conditions" justifies typing the two endpoints of a causal
  edge as the *initial* quality (set-up) and *resulting* quality (termination) of the
  abbreviated process.
- Cross-scale: MDC mechanisms are intrinsically multilevel (activities of lower-level
  entities constitute higher-level behaviors) — Craver's later "constitutive
  relevance" is the philosophical root of PhysioMap's CONSTITUTIVE cross-scale edges.
