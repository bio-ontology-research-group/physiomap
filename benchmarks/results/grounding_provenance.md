# Cell-of-origin grounding — provenance manifest  *** DRAFT FOR REVIEW ***

This manifest records the **constitutive `production` lifts** that ground each secreted /
synthesised macro substance in its **cell of origin**, closing the "free-floating macro"
gap. Each lift is a cross-scale constitutive edge `producer_cell ▷ substance` (relation
`production`, sign `+`): the producing cell's secretion/synthesis rate *constitutes* (does
not causally regulate) the macro level. The producer cell is a **cellular-scale root**
(clamped under intervention, lifting upward only) — it adds **no downward macro→micro
causal edge**, so the meta-graph stays acyclic.

Per-edge provenance (textbook anchor + mechanism) lives in a YAML comment above each edge
in the owning fragment. Every cell IRI was **verified against EBI OLS4** (Cell Ontology)
before use; substances with no single dominant cell of origin were **left ungrounded by
design** (not fabricated) — see "Intentionally ungrounded" below.

Textbook anchors used across the groundings: Boron & Boulpaep *Medical Physiology*;
Guyton & Hall *Textbook of Medical Physiology*; *Williams Textbook of Endocrinology*;
Janeway's *Immunobiology* (cytokines). These are sufficient, canonical cell-of-origin
facts; primary PMIDs are cited only where an author was certain.

## Intentionally ungrounded (no single dominant cell of origin — NOT fabricated)

- **angiotensin_II** — generated by enzymatic ACE cleavage of angiotensin I in
  plasma / on endothelium; no single secreting cell. (Left as a `derived` node.)
- **free_t3** — arises largely from peripheral 5′-deiodination of T4 in many tissues,
  not a single secreting cell.
- **somatostatin** (hypothalamic) — the Cell Ontology has no hypothalamus-specific
  somatostatinergic neuron distinct from the pancreatic δ-cell; left ungrounded rather
  than conflate the two sources. Revisit if CL adds the specific term.
- **thrombin / plasmin / activated_protein_C** — enzymatically activated from circulating
  zymogens in the coagulation/fibrinolysis cascade (they have causal parents), not a
  single cell secretion.
- **Pools / ratios / organ mechanics** (plasma Na⁺/K⁺/glucose/iron, osmolality,
  hematocrit, viscosity, transferrin saturation, ejection fraction, gas tensions, pH,
  MAP …) — within-scale `derived` quantities, correctly *not* constituted.
