## Construction provenance

The remaining files document source extraction, grounding, intermediate
coverage analyses, and regression evaluations used during construction. Some
retain historical labels such as `draft` because they are frozen source
records cited by released axioms or release hashes. They are not presented as
current capabilities or manuscript results.
