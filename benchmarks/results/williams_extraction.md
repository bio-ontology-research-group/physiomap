# Williams endocrinology curation

*Williams Textbook of Endocrinology* was consulted through an institutional
library copy that is not redistributed.

Candidate relations were reviewed for directness, direction, context, and
relation type. Only claims satisfying the PhysioMap admission criteria were
retained. Evidence class, source location, and mechanism notes remain attached
to the accepted axioms.

The current curated content is in
[`benchmarks/human/curated/williams_extracted.yaml`](../human/curated/williams_extracted.yaml).
Raw source text and intermediate extraction transcripts are not part of the
public release.
