# Hall textbook curation

*Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology* was used as a curation source
through an institutional library copy that is not redistributed.

Candidate statements were reviewed against the PhysioMap evidence model.
Associations, descriptions, and ambiguous directional claims were not admitted
as causal influences. Accepted relations retain source, chapter or page
information, the applicable evidence class, and a mechanism note in the
curated axiom.

The current content derived from this source is in
[`benchmarks/human/curated/hall_extracted.yaml`](../human/curated/hall_extracted.yaml).
Raw extraction batches, full-text sections, and rejected candidate transcripts
are retained outside the public release.
