# West respiratory-physiology curation

*West's Respiratory Physiology* was consulted through an institutional library
copy that is not redistributed.

Statements were admitted only after relation-type and directional review.
Causal influences required interventional or mechanistic support, while
quantitative identities and constitutive constraints were represented under
their separate semantics. Every retained axiom carries its evidence and source
provenance.

The current curated content is in
[`benchmarks/human/curated/west_extracted.yaml`](../human/curated/west_extracted.yaml).
Raw source text and intermediate extraction transcripts are not part of the
public release.
